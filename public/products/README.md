# Product Photography Sourcing

These images are generic, unbranded stock photography sourced from Unsplash
(free for commercial use under the [Unsplash License](https://unsplash.com/license),
no attribution required). Each was deliberately chosen to show a generic
product form factor with **no visible real drug brand names, logos, or
packaging** — this catalog's products are fictional ("Meridian" house
brand), so imagery was picked to avoid implying affiliation with any real
pharmaceutical company.

**Every product has its own unique photo — no image is reused.** Each form
factor's pool size equals the number of products with that dosage form
(tablet 23, capsule 10, injection 6, cream 4, inhaler 2, drops 2, syrup 1,
kit 1, supplement 1 = 50), and `lib/data/products.ts` assigns each product a
distinct key from its form factor's pool. See
`components/products/product-image.tsx` (`POOL_SIZE`).

> **Note on specificity:** these are photos of *generic* pills, capsules,
> tubes, inhalers, and injection pens matched to each product's dosage form —
> they are not photographs of the specific named medicine. Meridian's product
> names are house-brand names, so no real-world packaging exists for them;
> using a real manufacturer's branded packaging under a Meridian name would
> misrepresent that manufacturer's product. Replace with real photography of
> the actual dispensed products before launch.
